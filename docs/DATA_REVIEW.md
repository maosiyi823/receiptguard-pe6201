## Checks and limitations

All 50 labels have been checked for amount/date-policy consistency without importing the tested policy implementation or running either extractor. Correct arithmetic does not independently establish semantic labels. Generation patterns are correlated, many layouts are simple, and only 12 cases contain the predeclared simulated noise. Production prevalence, real photographic/OCR performance and an exhaustive range of policies are unverified.

The original under-10-percent abstention target is inappropriate for five cases that must abstain. Report the mandatory abstention subset separately and measure unnecessary abstention on the other 45. Record this pre-evaluation design decision in the final report.
